Creamy Chicken Pasta

  30 mins   Serves 2   Dinner

Ingredients

  • 200g penne pasta
  • 250g chicken breast, diced
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 70g sun-dried tomatoes
  • 150ml double cream
  • 30g Parmesan cheese
  • Fresh basil
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta in salted boiling water according to the packet instructions. Reserve a little pasta water before draining.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a pan. Add the chicken and cook until golden and fully cooked through.
  3. Add the garlic and sun-dried tomatoes. Stir for approximately one minute.
  4. Reduce the heat and stir in the double cream and Parmesan until smooth.
  5. Add the cooked pasta and mix well. Use a little reserved pasta water if the sauce is too thick.
  6. Finish with fresh basil and serve warm.
